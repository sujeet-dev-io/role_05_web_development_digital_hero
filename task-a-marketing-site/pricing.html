FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes! All plans include a 14-day free trial with full access to features. No credit card required to start.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately and billing is prorated.

What counts as an execution?

An execution is one complete run of a workflow from trigger to final action. Multi-step workflows count as a single execution regardless of the number of steps.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

Yes. We offer 50% off for eligible startups (under $5M funding) and nonprofit organizations. Contact our sales team to apply.

What is your uptime guarantee?

We guarantee 99.9% uptime on all plans, backed by SLA credits. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom SLAs up to 99.99%.
